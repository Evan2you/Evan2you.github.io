III. Calculation — MDP

25 points

C1. A branching episodic MRP [8 points]

States are A,B,C,T, with T terminal and γ=0.5. A gives reward 1 and transitions to B or C with probability 0.5 each. B gives reward 0 and moves to C. C gives reward 4 and terminates.

(a) Write the transition matrix and reward vector in order (A,B,C,T). [2]

(b) Compute all returns in the sampled episode A → B → C → T. [2]

(c) Compute the exact values of C, B and A by backward substitution. [2]

(d) Starting at V₀=0, give synchronous V₁,V₂,V₃ for (A,B,C). [2]

C2. Risky action and policy averaging [8 points]

γ=0.5. At S, safe gives reward 2 and terminates; bold gives reward 5 and moves to T or L with probability 0.5 each. At L, quit gives reward 0 and terminates; recover gives reward -2 and returns to S. A policy chooses each available action with probability 0.5.

(a) Derive the two Bellman expectation equations. [2]

(b) Solve for vπ(S),vπ(L). [2]

(c) Calculate the four qπ values and a greedy improved policy. [2]

(d) Evaluate the improved policy and determine whether it is already optimal. Justify your answer by checking both actions at L. [2]

C3. Value iteration with stochastic outcomes [9 points]

γ=0.5. In S, action a gives reward 1 and moves to S or U with probability 0.5 each; action b gives reward 0 and terminates. In U, action c gives reward 2 and terminates; action d gives reward 0 and moves to S.

(a) Write both Bellman optimality backup formulas. [1]

(b) From V₀=(0,0), compute V₁. [2]

(c) Compute V₂. [2]

(d) Compute V₃ and the actions selected in this third backup. [2]

(e) Evaluate the stationary policy (a,c) exactly and check Bellman optimality. [2]

IV. Calculation — Learning algorithms

20 points

D1. MC, online TD and one frozen batch sweep [10 points]

Two episodes are observed in this order: (1) A --1→ B --2→ T; (2) A --2→ T. γ=1. For TD use α=0.5 and initial V(A)=V(B)=0. Independent methods reset to their specified initial conditions.

(a) List the return for each visit to A and B. [2]

(b) Compute first-visit sample-mean MC estimates using only these episodes. [2]

(c) Apply online TD(0) in the order the transitions were observed. Show all three updates. [2]

(d) Starting again at zero, perform ONE frozen batch TD sweep: compute TD errors using the initial zero table, average the errors separately for each state, then update each state once using α=0.5. [3]

(e) Explain why the two TD results need not agree. [1]

D2. Negative action values [10 points]

A nonterminal transition has Q(S,A)=-3, r=-2, γ=0.5 and α=0.2. Online next-state Q values (a₁,a₂,a₃) are (-2,-4,-1); the actual next action is a₁. Behaviour is ε-greedy with ε=0.2. Target-network values are (-3,-1,-2). Treat algorithms independently.

(a) Give the ε-greedy action probabilities. [2]

(b) Compute the SARSA target and update. [2]

(c) Compute the Q-learning target and update. [2]

(d) Compute DQN and Double DQN targets, identifying the selected actions. [3]

(e) Recompute the target and tabular update if this transition is terminal. [1]
