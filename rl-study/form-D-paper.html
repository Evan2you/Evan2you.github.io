III. Calculation — MDP

25 points

C1. Rewards correlated with transitions [8 points]

There is one nonterminal state S and one terminal state T. From S, with probability 0.5 the process returns to S and receives reward 2; with probability 0.5 it enters T and receives reward 6. γ=0.5.

(a) Find the expected immediate reward at S and write the Bellman equation. [2]

(b) Solve for v(S). [2]

(c) For S --2→ S --6→ T, compute both visit returns. [2]

(d) From V₀(S)=0, compute V₁,V₂,V₃ synchronously. [2]

C2. Nonuniform policy probabilities [8 points]

γ=0.5. At A, fast gives reward 1 and terminates; support gives reward 0 and moves to B. At B, finish gives reward 3 and terminates; repeat gives reward 1 and returns to B. π(fast|A)=0.25, π(support|A)=0.75; π chooses each B action with probability 0.5.

(a) Write Bellman expectation equations. [2]

(b) Solve for vπ(A),vπ(B). [2]

(c) Find all four qπ values and a greedy policy π′. [2]

(d) Evaluate π′ and verify whether another improvement is possible. [2]

C3. A random walk with two terminals [9 points]

Five locations lie on a line: Tₗ — L — M — R — Tᵣ. Both end locations are terminal. At each nonterminal state, left and right are chosen with probability 0.5 and move to the adjacent location. Every move costs -1, including entry into a terminal. γ=1.

(a) From V₀=0, give V₁ for (L,M,R). [1]

(b) Compute V₂ synchronously. [2]

(c) Compute V₃ synchronously. [2]

(d) Solve for the exact random-policy values. [2]

(e) Using those exact values, list all greedy actions in each state. [2]

D1. Targets of different horizons [10 points]

Observed episodes are (1) A --0→ B --2→ T; (2) A --2→ T. γ=1. For constant-step updates initialize V(A)=1,V(B)=0 and α=0.5. Treat methods independently.

(a) List all visit returns. [2]

(b) Compute first-visit sample-mean MC estimates from these episodes with fresh counts. [2]

(d) Starting from the original values, perform ONLY the two-step update for the first visit to A in episode 1. [2]

(e) Why does this two-step target equal the MC return for that visit? [1]

D2. Tie-breaking changes Double DQN [10 points]

(a) Compute the behaviour probabilities. [2]

(b) Compute SARSA's target and update. [2]

(c) Compute Q-learning's target and update. [2]

(d) Compute DQN and Double DQN targets, applying the stated tie rule. [3]
